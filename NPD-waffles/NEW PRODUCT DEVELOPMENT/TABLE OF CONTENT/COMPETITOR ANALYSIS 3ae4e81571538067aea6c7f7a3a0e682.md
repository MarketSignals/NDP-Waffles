## 🏪 EXISTING MARKET LANDSCAPE

The healthy dessert market has grown rapidly in recent years as consumers increasingly look for products that satisfy their sweet cravings without compromising their health goals. This expansion has led to a wide range of options, including sugar-free chocolates, healthier cookies, protein-rich snacks, and frozen waffles designed for convenience. Many of these products focus on reducing sugar, increasing protein, or using better-quality ingredients to appeal to health-conscious buyers. However, despite this variety, there is still a noticeable gap in the market. Very few products successfully combine a lower glycemic impact with the convenience of a ready-to-eat frozen dessert while also delivering an indulgent taste experience. Additionally, the frozen stuffed waffle format remains largely underdeveloped, even though it offers a unique combination of portability, texture, and flavor. A product that brings together these features could meet the needs of consumers seeking a healthier dessert that is both enjoyable and convenient. This represents a promising opportunity for innovation and differentiation in the growing healthy dessert industry.
