## 🎯 STP STRATEGY

### 📍 Segmentation

- Adults with Type 2 diabetes
- Prediabetic consumers
- Health-conscious individuals
- Working professionals
- Families seeking healthier dessert options

### 🎯 Targeting

The primary target market consists of adults managing diabetes and health-conscious consumers seeking convenient dessert options with a better nutritional profile. Secondary targets include families and working professionals looking for premium frozen snacks.

### ⭐ Positioning

A premium frozen stuffed waffle that delivers indulgent taste with a lower glycemic impact, allowing both diabetic and non-diabetic consumers to enjoy the same dessert experience.
