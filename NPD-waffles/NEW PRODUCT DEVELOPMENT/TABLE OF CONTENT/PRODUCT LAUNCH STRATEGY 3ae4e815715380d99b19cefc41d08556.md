# PRODUCT LAUNCH STRATEGY

> "A successful launch is not just about introducing a product—it is about creating awareness, generating trial, and building lasting consumer trust.”
> 

---

## 🎯 LAUNCH OBJECTIVES

<aside>

- Build strong brand awareness.
- Encourage first-time product trials.
- Establish retail and online availability.
- Generate positive consumer feedback.
- Create a foundation for long-term growth.
</aside>

---

## 📅 LAUNCH TIMELINE

| PHASE | ACTIVITY |
| --- | --- |
| MONTH 1 | Final product validation & packaging approval |
| MONTH 2 | Pilot production & retailer onboarding |
| MONTH 3 | Soft launch in selected cities and online channels |
| MONTH 4 | Consumer feedback collection & optimization |
| MONTH 5 - 6 | Expansion to additional retail locations and e-commerce platforms |
