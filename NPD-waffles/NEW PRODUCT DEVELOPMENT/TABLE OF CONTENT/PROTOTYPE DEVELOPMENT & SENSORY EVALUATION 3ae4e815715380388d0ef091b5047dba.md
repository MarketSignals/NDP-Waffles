## 🧪 PROTOTYPE DEVELOPMENT

A laboratory-scale prototype of the frozen stuffed waffle was developed to validate the feasibility of the proposed product formulation and processing method. All ingredients were accurately weighed and mixed according to the optimized formulation to obtain a uniform and stable waffle batter. During product development, the batter consistency was carefully adjusted to achieve the ideal viscosity, ensuring smooth pouring, even distribution within the waffle mold, and the desired texture after cooking. A measured quantity of chocolate filling was placed between two layers of batter before the cooking process to create a well-defined stuffed center with consistent filling distribution. The waffles were cooked until they achieved a golden-brown, crisp exterior while maintaining a soft and fluffy interior. After cooking, the products were allowed to cool completely to prevent moisture condensation during storage. The waffles were then individually packed in food-grade packaging, frozen under controlled conditions, and stored for subsequent evaluation of their sensory characteristics, texture, structural integrity, and storage stability. This prototype served as the foundation for further product optimization and quality assessment.

## 👅 SENSORY EVALUATION

The developed prototype was evaluated through a sensory assessment to determine its overall consumer acceptability and product quality. A structured sensory evaluation method was used to assess key attributes, including appearance, aroma, taste, texture, sweetness, and overall acceptability. Participants were provided with freshly prepared samples under controlled conditions and were asked to rate each attribute using a standardized hedonic scale. The evaluation focused on identifying the balance between the product's nutritional improvements and its sensory appeal, ensuring that the lower glycemic formulation did not compromise consumer satisfaction. The feedback obtained from the assessment was used to identify strengths, areas for improvement, and the overall market potential of the frozen stuffed waffle.
