## 🏷️ BRAND POSITIONING

The frozen stuffed waffle is positioned as a **premium functional frozen dessert** that delivers the perfect balance between indulgence and nutrition. Unlike conventional desserts that focus primarily on taste or products designed exclusively for individuals with diabetes, this product is intended for a broader consumer base seeking healthier yet satisfying dessert alternatives. Its positioning emphasizes guilt-free indulgence by offering a lower glycemic formulation, high-quality ingredients, and a convenient ready-to-heat format without compromising on flavor or texture. The brand is built around the values of **trust, quality, convenience, and modern healthy living**, appealing to health-conscious consumers, busy professionals, fitness enthusiasts, and families alike. By combining a crisp waffle exterior with a rich chocolate-filled center, the product delivers a premium café-style experience that can be enjoyed at home within minutes. This positioning differentiates the product from both traditional frozen desserts and health-focused snacks, creating a unique space in the market where consumers can enjoy an indulgent treat while making a more mindful dietary choice.

## 🌱 SUSTAINABLE PACKAGING

<aside>

The proposed packaging has been designed with sustainability in mind by utilizing **recyclable paperboard** for the outer carton while minimizing the use of plastic wherever technically feasible. This approach helps reduce environmental impact without compromising the product's quality, safety, or shelf life. The packaging is intended to provide adequate protection against moisture, contamination, and physical damage during storage and transportation while maintaining the frozen product's integrity. In line with growing consumer demand for environmentally responsible packaging, future product development will explore the use of **compostable, biodegradable, or bio-based packaging materials** as sustainable alternatives. Any new packaging solution will be carefully evaluated to ensure it maintains essential functional properties such as barrier performance, durability, food safety, and compatibility with frozen storage conditions. By integrating sustainable packaging practices with product innovation, the brand aims to reduce its environmental footprint while delivering a premium, convenient, and environmentally conscious frozen dessert experience.

</aside>
