## 📖 Background

Diabetes has emerged as one of the most significant global public health challenges, with its prevalence increasing steadily due to changing lifestyles, unhealthy dietary habits, physical inactivity, and population aging. The rising incidence of type 2 diabetes has heightened awareness of the importance of maintaining balanced blood glucose levels through healthier food choices. As consumers become more health-conscious, there is a growing demand for foods that support better nutrition while reducing the risk of diet-related chronic diseases.

Among these changing preferences, desserts present a unique challenge. Although consumers recognize the need to limit sugar intake, they continue to seek indulgent foods that provide enjoyment, comfort, and sensory satisfaction. Traditional desserts are often high in refined sugar and calories, making them less suitable for individuals managing diabetes or those pursuing healthier lifestyles. This has created a demand for alternatives that preserve the taste, texture, and appeal of conventional desserts while offering improved nutritional value.

Food innovation plays a vital role in addressing these evolving consumer needs by developing products that combine health benefits with indulgence. The incorporation of natural sweeteners, functional ingredients, dietary fiber, and low-glycemic formulations has enabled the creation of healthier dessert options without compromising quality or consumer acceptance. Such innovations contribute to better dietary choices and support the growing market for functional and health-oriented food products.

## 🎯 PROJECT AIM

The primary objective of this project is to develop a **frozen stuffed waffle** with a **lower glycemic impact** while maintaining excellent **taste, texture, and overall consumer acceptance**. The product is designed to provide a convenient, ready-to-cook breakfast or snack option that supports healthier eating habits without compromising on sensory appeal. By carefully selecting ingredients and optimizing the formulation, the aim is to create a nutritious, innovative waffle that delivers balanced nutrition, enjoyable eating quality, and strong market potential.
