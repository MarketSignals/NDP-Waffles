# WHY I CHOSE THIS TOPIC

I chose to develop a center-filled frozen waffle because this project is personally meaningful to me. My grandfather has diabetes, and although he loves sweets, he often has to avoid many desserts that the rest of our family enjoys.

This inspired me to create a dessert that is both enjoyable and more suitable for people who need to be mindful of their sugar intake. My goal was to develop a center-filled frozen waffle that allows my grandfather to enjoy a delicious treat without feeling left out.

I believe that food is more than just nutrition—it brings families together. I wanted to create a product that everyone in my family could enjoy together, so that instead of serving him a different dessert, we could all share the same one. This project reflects my desire to combine innovation, taste, and inclusivity, ensuring that people with dietary restrictions can still be part of special family moments.

For me, this project is not just about creating a frozen waffle; it is about creating memories, promoting togetherness, and showing care through food.
