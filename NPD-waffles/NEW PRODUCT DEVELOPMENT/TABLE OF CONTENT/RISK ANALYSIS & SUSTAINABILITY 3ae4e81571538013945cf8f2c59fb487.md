## ⚠️ RISK OVERVIEW

Every new product carries a range of **technical, commercial, and operational risks** that may influence its successful development and market performance. Technical risks may include challenges related to product formulation, maintaining consistent texture after freezing, shelf-life stability, and production scalability. Commercial risks involve factors such as consumer acceptance, competitive offerings, pricing strategies, and changing market preferences. Operational risks may arise from raw material availability, manufacturing efficiency, cold chain maintenance, and distribution logistics. Identifying these risks at an early stage enables proactive planning and the implementation of appropriate mitigation strategies, reducing the likelihood of costly delays or product failures. Regular product testing, quality assurance, market research, and supply chain planning help minimize uncertainties throughout the product lifecycle. Alongside risk management, sustainability plays a vital role in ensuring long-term business success. The use of responsibly sourced ingredients, recyclable packaging, efficient manufacturing practices, and reduced food waste strengthens environmental performance while meeting growing consumer expectations. These sustainable practices enhance brand reputation, build consumer trust, and improve long-term competitiveness in the rapidly evolving frozen food market.
