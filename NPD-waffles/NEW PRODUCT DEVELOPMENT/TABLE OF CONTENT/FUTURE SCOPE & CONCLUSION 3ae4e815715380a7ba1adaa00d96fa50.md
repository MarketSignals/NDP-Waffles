## PROJECT AT A GLANCE

| CATEGORY | SUMMARY |
| --- | --- |
| 🧇 **Product** | Frozen Stuffed Waffle with a premium stuffed filling and a lower glycemic impact. |
| 🎯 **Problem Addressed** | Limited availability of indulgent desserts suitable for both diabetic and non-diabetic consumers. |
| 💡 **Core Innovation** | Combines convenience, indulgence, and a better nutritional profile in a frozen ready-to-heat format. |
| 👥 **Target Consumers** | Adults with Type 2 Diabetes, Prediabetic Consumers, Health-Conscious Individuals, Working Professionals, and Families. |
| 🌟 **Unique Value Proposition** | A premium frozen dessert that allows everyone to enjoy the same sweet treat without compromising on taste or convenience. |
| 🥣 **Key Ingredients** | Oat flour, almond flour, whole wheat flour, erythritol, stevia, inulin, whey protein, butter, vanilla, and dark chocolate filling. |
| 🏭 **Manufacturing Process** | Ingredient preparation → Batter mixing → Stuffing preparation → Waffle baking → Cooling → Rapid freezing → Packaging → Frozen storage. |
| 📦 **Packaging** | Premium paperboard frozen carton with individual food-grade wrappers and clear preparation instructions. |
| 🛡️ **Food Safety** | GMP, GHP, HACCP principles, temperature monitoring, allergen management, and FSSAI-compliant labeling. |
| 📣 **Marketing Strategy** | STP approach supported by digital marketing, influencer collaborations, retail sampling, and omnichannel distribution. |
| 💰 **Financial Feasibility** | Premium value-based pricing supported by scalable production, positive gross margins, and commercial growth potential. |
| 🚀 **Launch Strategy** | Phased launch beginning with pilot markets, followed by retail expansion, digital promotion, and consumer feedback integration. |
| ⚠️ **Major Risks** | Consumer acceptance, ingredient cost fluctuations, cold-chain dependency, competition, and regulatory compliance. |
| 🌱 **Sustainability** | Recyclable packaging (where feasible), reduced food waste through frozen storage, and responsible manufacturing practices. |
| 📈 **Future Opportunities** | New flavors, formulation improvements, national and international expansion, D2C growth, and strategic partnerships. |
| 🏆 **Project Outcome** | Demonstrates the successful application of New Product Development principles to create a commercially viable frozen stuffed waffle with strong market potential. |
