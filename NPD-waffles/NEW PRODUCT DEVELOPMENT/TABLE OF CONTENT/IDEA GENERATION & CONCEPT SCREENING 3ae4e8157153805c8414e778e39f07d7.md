## 🧠 IDEA GENERATION

The idea generation stage focused on developing a dessert that satisfies sweet cravings while providing a lower glycemic impact. Multiple product concepts were evaluated based on consumer preferences, convenience, nutritional value, production feasibility, and market potential. After comparing the alternatives, a **frozen stuffed waffle** was selected as the most promising concept due to its unique combination of indulgence, convenience, longer shelf life, and potential for healthier formulation.

---

| Chocolate Lava Mug Cake | Premium experience | Short shelf life |
| Frozen Pancakes | Convenient | Limited differentiation |
