## 📝 THE PROBLEM

Diabetes is becoming an increasingly serious global health concern, with the number of affected individuals rising steadily due to unhealthy dietary habits, sedentary lifestyles, and obesity. Managing blood glucose levels requires long-term dietary modifications, yet one of the greatest challenges for many people is controlling sweet cravings. The natural preference for sweet-tasting foods often makes it difficult to completely eliminate sugary treats from the diet, leading to poor adherence to healthier eating patterns.

Traditional desserts are typically prepared with refined sugar and rapidly digestible carbohydrates, which can cause sharp increases in blood glucose levels and are therefore unsuitable for individuals with diabetes or those at risk of developing the condition. Although avoiding desserts altogether may help reduce sugar intake, it can also decrease meal satisfaction and limit the enjoyment associated with eating, making long-term dietary compliance more difficult. Therefore, there is a growing need to develop healthier indulgent alternatives that provide the sensory appeal of conventional desserts while incorporating ingredients with a lower glycemic impact, improved nutritional value, and reduced sugar content. Such products can help satisfy sweet cravings without compromising health, supporting better dietary adherence and contributing to improved diabetes prevention and management.

## 💡 MARKET OPPORTUNITY

The growing prevalence of diabetes, combined with the persistent demand for sweet foods, creates a significant opportunity to develop healthier dessert alternatives. By replacing refined sugar and rapidly digestible carbohydrates with low-glycemic, nutrient-rich ingredients, it is possible to offer products that satisfy consumers' desire for indulgence while supporting better blood glucose management. Such innovations can meet the needs of individuals with diabetes, health-conscious consumers, and those seeking balanced dietary choices, making healthier desserts an increasingly attractive and expanding market segment.
