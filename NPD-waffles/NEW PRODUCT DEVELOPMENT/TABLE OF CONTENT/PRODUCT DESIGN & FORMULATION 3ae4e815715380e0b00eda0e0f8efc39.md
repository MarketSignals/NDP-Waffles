# PRODUCT DESIGN & FORMULATION

> "A successful food product is created by balancing nutrition, functionality, taste, texture, and consumer expectations.”
> 

---

## 🧇 PRODUCT OVERVIEW

The product is a frozen stuffed waffle developed to deliver an indulgent eating experience while maintaining a lower glycemic impact compared with conventional alternatives. It is intended for both diabetic and non-diabetic consumers seeking a convenient, better-for-you snack or breakfast option without compromising on taste. The frozen format enhances convenience, preserves freshness, and extends shelf life, making it easy to store and prepare whenever needed.

## 🥣 INGREDIENT SELECTION

| INGREDIENT | FUNCTION IN PTODUCT | WHY SELECTED |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oat Flour | Base flour | Rich in β-glucan fiber, contributes to lower glycemic response |
| Almond Flour | Texture & nutrition | Healthy fats, protein, lower carbohydrate content |
| Whole Wheat Flour | Structure | Improves texture and handling |
| Erythritol | Sweetener | Sweet taste with negligible calories |
| Stevia | High-intensity sweetener | Enhances sweetness without added sugar |
| Inulin | Dietary fiber | Improves fiber content and mouthfeel |
| Whey Protein | Protein enrichment | Better nutritional profile |
| Butter | Fat source | Rich flavor and crisp texture |
| Baking Powder | Leavening | Creates light waffle texture |
| Vanilla Extract | Flavor | Enhances aroma |
| Dark Chocolate Filling | Stuffing | Premium taste and indulgence |
