# 🧇 New Product Development (NPD) – Waffles

> MBA Academic Project documenting the complete **New Product Development (NPD)** lifecycle for a waffle product.

---

# 📖 Project Overview

This repository presents the complete process of developing a new waffle product, covering:

- Market Opportunity Identification
- Consumer & Market Research
- Competitor Analysis
- Product Design
- Branding & Packaging
- Manufacturing Process
- Prototype Development
- Marketing Strategy
- Financial Feasibility
- Product Launch Strategy
- Risk Analysis
- Future Scope
- References
