## 📖 Project Overview

This repository contains the complete documentation for a **New Product Development (NPD)** project focused on the development of a waffle-based product.

The project follows the standard NPD framework, covering:

- 🔍 Market Opportunity Analysis
- 👥 Consumer & Market Research
- 📊 Competitor Analysis
- 💡 Idea Generation
- 🧪 Product Design & Formulation
- 🎨 Branding & Packaging
- 🏭 Manufacturing Process
- 🍽️ Prototype Development
- 📣 Marketing Strategy
- 💰 Financial Feasibility
- ⚠️ Risk Assessment
- 🚀 Product Launch Strategy
- 📈 Future Scope
